Good to know

Before you download.

Is Weather Live available on Galaxy Store now?

This site is live, but the new Galaxy Store listing is still being prepared. The badge above points to the planned package address and may show an unavailable page until publication.

Does the app include an embedded weather radar?

The current app offers an external weather-map link. Embedded radar is unavailable until a compatible provider is connected.

Are ads or Premium purchases active?

Advertising is currently disabled. Original scenic artwork is locked for Premium, and purchases are not yet available for this new app package.
