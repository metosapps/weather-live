Privacy Policy

Effective 23 October 2026

This policy explains how the Weather Live Android application (com.metosapps.weatherlive) and this website handle information. Contact alendatofall@gmail.com for privacy questions or requests.

Information used

  • Location and places: With your permission, precise or approximate device location may be used to show local weather. Places you search for or save, coordinates, region and time zone may be kept on your device and sent to weather, geocoding and radar services. You can deny location access and use manual place search where supported.
  • Weather requests: Weather services receive the requested location or coordinates and normal connection details such as your IP address. The app currently calls services hosted at swtapi.tohapp.com, airapi.tohapp.com and sradar.tohapp.com. Embedded maps may load additional provider content.
  • Preferences and diagnostics: The app stores settings, saved places, cached forecasts, notification choices and consent choices locally. Integrated SDKs may process device details, identifiers, usage events, errors and performance data. Weather data and provider availability can change.
  • Notifications: If enabled, weather alerts and scheduled updates may use your saved locations, device notification permission and a provider messaging token. You can turn notifications off in Android settings.
  • Advertising: Development builds use Google's demo/test ad identifiers only. Even test ads can involve network requests and device or app data processed by Google Mobile Ads. The app offers an advertising privacy options entry when a regional form is available. No production ad unit is configured for this package.
  • Support and purchases: Emailing us provides your address and message to us and your email provider. If paid features are introduced later, Galaxy Store will process payment; the app would receive purchase status and product identifiers, not your full card number. No Premium products are currently offered for sale for this new package.

Providers and purposes

We use weather services to deliver forecasts and maps, Google services for advertising consent, test advertising and app operation, Samsung services for Galaxy distribution, and GitHub Pages for this website and public app settings. Their services may log connection details and process data under their own policies. Read Google's Privacy Policy, Samsung's Privacy Policy, and GitHub's Privacy Statement.

The public remote settings file controls only supported ad placements and app links. It does not receive your saved locations, purchase receipts or email address in its request body. This website uses no analytics scripts, forms or tracking cookies.

Your choices and rights

You may manage location and notification permissions in Android, edit or delete saved locations where available, clear app storage, or uninstall the app. If an advertising privacy form is required in your region, the app can present it and provide an entry to reopen available choices. These actions do not erase data already retained by independent providers. Depending on your location, you may request access, correction or deletion of information within our control, object to certain processing, or withdraw consent. Email alendatofall@gmail.com with the app name and your request; do not send passwords or payment card details.

Retention, security and children

Local data remains until replaced or removed. Support correspondence and service records may be kept as needed for support, security or legal obligations. Provider retention periods vary. Network requests use HTTPS where available, but no online service can promise perfect security. Weather Live is a general-audience app and is not specifically designed for children. Parents or guardians should supervise use where required.

Changes

We may update this policy as features and services change. The effective date above identifies this version. The app includes an offline copy and a link to this current online version.
